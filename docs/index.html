what it is

Gander's aim is full goose embodiment. What is that? Your head is the goose's. Your arms are its wings, and if you like, you can actually honk to honk. Or yell any expletive of your choice, which is what I imagine geese are actually saying when they are being horrible.

If you'd like a less embodied experience, you can switch to a tabletop view, which lets you look down on yourself in third person.

Local two-player mode is still supported, with one player on keyboard and mouse or a controller and the other in VR. Your movements are mirrored on your flatscreen goose.

getting it

You need Windows, Untitled Goose Game (preferably on Steam, though other installs should work), and a PCVR headset. Steam should be running if you want achievements.

  1. Download Gander. It's just the one file.
  2. Run it. It finds the game, installs everything it needs under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Gander, and adds a shortcut.
  3. Put the headset on and start Gander from the shortcut. It starts the game by itself after a short count.

Nothing is written into the game folder. Starting anywhere but the launcher loads the flat game. Uninstall in "Add or remove programs".
